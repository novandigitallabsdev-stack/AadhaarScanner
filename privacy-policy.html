This Application is published and maintained by an Individual developer located in Thane, Maharashtra, India. Our core technical architecture is engineered around the principle of complete offline data sovereignty and zero server collection.

Foundational Privacy Guarantee
We do not operate remote databases, cloud synchronization servers, or external tracking backends. All operations, record processing, and identity scanning take place entirely on the user's local mobile device.
02

Personal Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal information from the users.

When you use Aadhaar Scanner For Lodges, neither the developer nor any affiliated server receives, intercepts, harvests, or stores:

  • User Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, personal phone number, private email address, or online credentials to use the application.
  • Lodge Guest Records: Any guest details entered, scanned, or imported (including guest names, room allocations, check-in dates, masked Aadhaar numbers, or contact phone numbers) exist exclusively within your device's isolated SQLite database sandbox (CustomerScansDB.db).
  • Identity Images: Photographs of Aadhaar cards, Passports, Driving Licenses, or other identity proofs captured using the App stay strictly in the device's internal storage directories. They are never transmitted over the Internet to us or to third parties.
  • Biometric Data: The application does not collect, capture, store, or transmit raw biometric templates (such as fingerprints or iris scans).
  • Location Data: The App does not access, record, or track your physical GPS coordinates or cellular location data.

04

Aadhaar Data Protection & UIDAI Compliance

The Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) and applicable Indian legal frameworks (including the Aadhaar Act, 2016, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) mandate stringent safeguards for handling Aadhaar credentials. Aadhaar Scanner For Lodges enforces compliance through native architectural protections:

  • Strict Aadhaar Masking: In strict compliance with UIDAI regulations, the application never displays or stores raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers. The first 8 digits are automatically masked (e.g., XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-1234), leaving only the permissible last 4 digits visible.
  • Offline Cryptographic Verification: Secure QR codes printed on authentic Aadhaar cards are digitally signed by UIDAI. The application parses and decompresses this signed data locally on the device to extract official text fields (Name, Date of Birth, Address) without contacting any external servers.
  • Name Immutability: To prevent fraudulent alteration of hotel records, the guest name verified cryptographically from the Aadhaar QR code is protected against unauthorized tampering.
  • Statutory Lodge Compliance: Under the Sarais Act and state police reporting mandates, commercial lodges must preserve guest registers. The application facilitates local 7-year record retention entirely on the lodge's offline device.

Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

Because all guest records and scan logs are stored exclusively in your local device storage, you maintain 100% sovereign ownership and control over data retention and data deletion:

  • Statutory 7-Year Retention: To comply with Indian lodging regulations (Sarais Act & state police guest register mandates), the application includes built-in safeguards to preserve guest records for the mandatory 7-year duration.
  • Manual Offline Backup: You can export full encrypted database backups and document image packages to offline storage media (such as physical USB thumb drives or external hard drives) via Android's Storage Access Framework. These backup files remain under your physical custody.
  • Complete Data Deletion: You may permanently erase individual customer records directly within the app's interface (subject to statutory purge rules). Furthermore, uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Data / Clear Storage" in Android Device Settings immediately and permanently destroys all local databases, images, and configuration records.
11

Information Security Safeguards

Although the application operates locally, we implement advanced software engineering and security practices to safeguard data on your device:

  • Application Sandboxing: All SQLite database files and guest identity scans are housed in Android's private internal storage sandbox, isolated from other third-party apps on the device.
  • Google Play Integrity Verification: The App leverages Google Play Integrity certification to ensure that only authentic, untampered binaries downloaded from the Google Play Store can run, protecting your lodge records against malicious sideloaded APK clones.
  • No Unencrypted Network Transmission: Because there is no cloud synchronization backend, sensitive guest records can never be intercepted in transit or exposed via external data breaches.
