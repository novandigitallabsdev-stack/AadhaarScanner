Govt of India & UIDAI Warning: Physical Photocopies (Xerox) Are Banned

Under current UIDAI circulars and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, commercial lodges and hotels are strictly prohibited from retaining unmasked physical photocopies of Aadhaar cards. Storing unmasked 12-digit Aadhaar xerox copies creates severe regulatory and criminal liabilities for hoteliers.

Government guidelines explicitly mandate that only Masked Aadhaar (where the first 8 digits are completely redacted) may be accepted and stored digitally by lodging operators.

Critical Notice for Hoteliers

DigiLocker QR is Not Valid for Lodges.
Use the New Official Aadhaar App.

Many guests attempt to present DigiLocker QR codes at check-in. However, DigiLocker QR codes lack the cryptographic lodging verification payload required under Indian lodging guidelines.

To resolve this, UIDAI launched the brand-new official Aadhaar App with secure offline verification QR codes. AadhaarScannerForLodges is calibrated specifically to work flawlessly with the new UIDAI Aadhaar App!

Pro Tip on Scanning Complex Aadhaar QR Codes

An Aadhaar QR Code is extremely dense: it embeds the guest's high-resolution portrait photograph (Keiji JP2 format), complete address, demographic fields, and UIDAI's 2048-bit digital signature. Ensure the guest's printed card is clean and unblurry, or ask them to display the QR on high screen brightness in the new Aadhaar App for instantaneous 1-second decoding.
